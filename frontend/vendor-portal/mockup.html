The vendor area of the business app

Parts sellers work at the counter computer, so the portal (web.carpals.ir/<locale>/vendor) is desktop-first. The pages a seller opens from a push on the phone (inquiries, approvals, trade requests) are built phone-first inside the same installable portal; there is no separate compact app (VND-GD-02). The buyer side is already live in the consumer PWA. Plan: frontend/vendor-dev-plan.json.
