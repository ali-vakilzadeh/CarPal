Two surfaces of one business app

The full provider portal (web.carpals.ir, desktop first) keeps every P- screen of Docs/90 §6. The compact CarPal Workshop PWA (web.carpals.ir/<locale>/workshop, installed on the phone) does three jobs for workshops without a computer: answer appointment requests, reply to customers, post before/after photos. Everything else opens the full portal. Plan: frontend/provider-dev-plan.json.
