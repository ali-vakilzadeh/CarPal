CarPal is operated by \[LEGAL ENTITY NAME\], \[REGISTERED ADDRESS\] ("CarPal", "we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use the CarPal apps for iOS and Android, the website at \[DOMAIN\], and the Provider, Vendor and Fleet portals (together, the "Service").

**Regional rules.** The core of this policy applies everywhere. Section 11 adds rules for specific regions: the Iran pilot, the EEA and UK, and the USA. Where an addendum gives you more protection, the addendum applies.

**Definitions.** "Personal information" means information about an identified or identifiable person. On CarPal this can include a VIN or license plate when it is linked to you. Terms such as "Service Provider", "Vendor" and "Fleet Organization" have the meanings given in the User Agreement.

## 5. AI features and automated processing

CarPal uses AI in the background to help you describe problems, find the right expert and keep records tidy. AI suggests; people decide.

**What AI does on CarPal**

- **For car owners and fleets:** Help Me triage, search interpretation (including Persian, English and Persian typed in Latin letters), provider matching and its explanation, maintenance suggestions, vehicle profile suggestions and pre-visit summaries.
- **For service providers:** profile and capability suggestions, the private skill heatmap, review response drafts, improvement tips, appointment slot suggestions, and showcase captions with suggestions to blur plates and faces.
- **For vendors:** cleaning up item names, categories and fitment, duplicate detection and inventory gap suggestions.
- **For platform safety:** detecting fake reviews, fraud, duplicate listings and content that breaks our rules.

**How we limit AI's access**

1. All AI features go through one governed layer that checks permissions and consent before any data is used.
2. Each task receives only the context it needs. Contact details are removed unless the task requires them.
3. One vendor's private inventory or trade prices are never used to benefit another vendor.
4. AI output is stored separately and labeled as AI-generated. It is never silently mixed with your own data.
5. Every AI action is logged, including the model version and whether a person approved it.
6. Nothing AI writes becomes public until a person approves it. AI never publishes a review response, showcase post or profile claim by itself.

**AI model providers.** We may use third-party AI model providers to run these features. They act only on our instructions, under contract, and may not use your information to train their own models. AI processing takes place in \[AI PROCESSING LOCATION\].

**Automated decisions.** Ranking, matching and fraud signals are computed automatically. They can affect where a business appears in results and how much weight a review carries. A person, not an algorithm alone, decides whether to remove content, reject a review, withhold a badge or suspend an account. You can ask us to explain a decision and to have a person review it.

**Limits of AI.** AI suggestions can be wrong. Help Me is not a diagnosis, and a maintenance suggestion is not a manufacturer instruction. Safety warnings are shown for symptoms such as soft brakes or loose steering. Follow them and have the vehicle inspected by a professional.

**Your controls.** In Settings > AI preferences you can turn off personalization based on your vehicle history. You can always search directly without using Help Me. Businesses can ignore any AI draft or suggestion. If AI is unavailable or switched off, search, appointments and records still work.

## 7. Location and maps

- **Precise location needs your permission.** We use it to find nearby businesses, sort by distance and center the map. It goes only to our search service. We do not keep a history of your movements. Search logs store only a rounded location of about \[1 km\]. Without permission, the map opens on a city you choose.
- **Map tiles and address search run on our own servers.** They are built from OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors). The part of the map you are viewing is not sent to a third-party map company.
- **Directions open another app.** When you tap Directions, we open the map app you choose, such as Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze or a regional app. We send it only the destination, never your identity or your vehicle. That app's own privacy policy then applies. Your choice of app is remembered on your device.
- **Distances are approximate.** CarPal shows straight-line distances. The external app shows the real route.
- **No vehicle or driver tracking.** CarPal does not track the location of vehicles or drivers. A driver can choose to add a location when reporting an issue, so the fleet can find help nearby.
- **Business locations are public.** Provider and vendor addresses and map pins are shown on their profiles. Home-based and mobile providers can show an approximate area instead of an exact address. Mobile providers show their service radius.
- **Photo locations are removed.** We strip location and camera data (EXIF) from photos when they are uploaded.

## 10. Security, transfers, children, cookies and changes

We protect information with encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access with least privilege for staff, audit logs, secure file uploads with malware scanning, rate limits and anti-scraping controls. Fleet data and vendor inventories are isolated per organization. AI tools use narrowly scoped access tokens. Two-step sign-in is available for every account and required for CarPal staff.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires, without undue delay. Please use a strong, unique password and do not share your account.
