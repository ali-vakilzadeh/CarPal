## 5. Search, appointments and part inquiries

### 5.1 How results are ranked

CarPal ranks Service Providers by how well their proven expertise fits your vehicle and problem, not only by distance. The main parameters are:

1. **Capability fit:** whether the provider lists your brand, model, series and the service needed.
2. **Evidence:** verified jobs, before/after media and reviews that prove that work.
3. **Rating** in the relevant service category, and how recent the evidence is.
4. **Trust signals:** verification status, response rate, appointment reliability and dispute history.
5. **Availability and location:** whether the provider can take the job and serves your area.
6. **Your preferences:** favorites, and a fleet's preferred providers.

Vendors and parts are ranked by fitment confidence, stock, distance, rating, price visibility, response time and data completeness.

Every recommendation shows the main reasons it appears. When a vehicle is selected, results are filtered to that vehicle; you can switch the filter off.

**No paid ranking.** In the MVP, no Business can pay to rank higher, appear in recommendations or appear on the map. If paid placements are added later, they will be clearly labeled as sponsored, kept separate from trust-based results, never shown as map pins, and introduced only after we update this Agreement with notice.

### 5.2 Appointments

- An appointment request is not a booking until the Service Provider confirms it. The provider may propose another time.
- When you send a request, the provider receives a Pre-Visit Report. Share only what you are comfortable sharing; you choose whether to include service history.
- Keep your appointments or cancel in the app as early as you can. The provider's own cancellation policy, if any, is shown before you send a request. Repeated no-shows may limit your ability to send requests.
- Estimates, prices, payment, warranty and the work itself are agreed between you and the provider. Ask for a written estimate before work starts.
- In a Fleet Organization, a driver's request may need a manager's approval before it is sent.

### 5.3 Part inquiries

- An inquiry or reservation is not a purchase. The Vendor confirms availability, price and fitment.
- Fitment is labeled by confidence and source, such as vendor-confirmed or AI-suggested. Confirm fitment with the Vendor or your mechanic before you buy or install a part.
- Prices and stock shown can change until the Vendor confirms them.
- Trade prices are shown only to authorized trade accounts. They are confidential and must not be shared.

### 5.4 Distances and directions

Distances are straight-line and approximate. Directions open a third-party map app, whose terms then apply.

## 6. Reviews

Reviews on CarPal must come from real customers. Every published review is confirmed in one of two ways, and a review confirmed in neither way is never published. A rating is never a reason to reject a review.

### 6.1 The two confirmation paths

|  | Path 1: Visit-based | Path 2: Approval-based |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When it applies | You had an appointment through CarPal that was confirmed and took place (not cancelled or a no-show), or other evidence links you to a real visit, such as a confirmed service record or checked invoice. | No appointment through CarPal (a walk-in, work arranged elsewhere, a past customer), or you write after the 7-day invitation window. |
| How it is confirmed | Automatic safety and abuse checks. The Business cannot approve or block it. | The Business confirms only that you were its customer, within 7 days. No answer within 7 days counts as no objection. A CarPal reviewer then approves it. |
| Label shown | Verified visit | Approved by the business and CarPal |
| Weight in scores | Full weight | Lower weight, as set in our scoring rules |

- You can review a visit-based appointment once it is completed, or 24 hours after its scheduled time if the provider has not closed it.
- On Path 2, the Business is asked only "Do you recognize this customer?". It sees your display name, vehicle type, service and approximate visit date, not your contact details. It cannot comment on or reject your opinion.
- If the Business says you were not its customer, the review goes to dispute. You can add evidence, such as an invoice or photo, and a CarPal reviewer decides. If the review is upheld, it is published with the note "Disputed by the business".

### 6.2 Review invitations

- After a completed appointment or a job the Business confirms, you may receive an invitation in the app and, if you agreed, by email.
- An email invitation contains a unique link for one visit and one person. It works once and expires 7 days after it is sent. You confirm your contact with a one-time code before submitting. Do not forward, share or sell invitation links.
- After 7 days, you can still review from the Business's profile under Path 2.

### 6.3 Rules for reviews

- Review only your own, first-hand experience as a customer, or your Fleet Organization's experience.
- Write one review per visit or inquiry. You may update or delete it.
- Do not review your own business, your employer, a relative's business or a competitor.
- Do not offer, ask for or accept money, discounts, gifts or other benefits for writing, changing or removing a review.
- Businesses must not invite only customers they expect to be happy, or pressure anyone over a review.
- You may use tools to help with wording, but the review must describe your genuine experience.
- Do not include other people's personal information, such as staff surnames, phone numbers or license plates.

### 6.4 Business responses

Businesses may reply publicly to a review, using AI drafts if they wish. Replies must be professional, must not reveal the customer's private information and must not threaten or retaliate. A Business may report a review that breaks these rules or request mediation, but may not demand removal of honest criticism.

### 6.5 How we check and moderate reviews

- We run automatic checks for patterns of fake reviews, such as sudden bursts, repeated wording, shared devices and self-reviews. Flagged reviews may carry lower weight while a person checks them.
- We remove a review only if it breaks this Agreement or the law, and we record the reason. We do not remove or hide reviews because they are negative, and Businesses cannot pay to remove or hide them.
- Nothing in this Agreement stops you from posting an honest review, and we will not penalize you for doing so.
