# CarPal MVP — Trust Scoring & Capability Confidence Protocol

**Complies with:** [02.Detailed project plan.md](02.Detailed%20project%20plan.md) (WP8, WP9, WP18), [50.MVP data model spec.md](50.MVP%20data%20model%20spec.md) and [52.MVP Data Dictionary.md](52.MVP%20Data%20Dictionary.md). Entity and field names below are the ones defined in Doc 52.

**Who runs it:** the `trust-scoring` worker (trust module, WP9) computes evidence, confidence, trust scores and badges. The discovery module (WP8) computes match scores. AI (WP18) can only supply inputs as described in §2.2 and §9.

**Calibration:** all weights, thresholds and penalties here are **launch defaults**. They are tuned during "trust scoring calibration" in release Stage 7 (Plan WP21). Every change gets a new `rule_version`.

---

# 1. Evidence

Evidence is the atomic unit of trust. Each evidence item is a `ProviderCapabilityEvidence` row linked to one `ProviderCapability` (brand / model / series / service system / service task).

## 1.1 Evidence types and base weights

| `evidence_type` | Base weight | Counts only when | Taxonomy context required |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `completed_appointment` | 10 | `Appointment.status = completed` | Vehicle + service system (task if known) |
| `verified_review` (5 stars) | 8 × trust weight | Review `published` and `counts_toward_core_score = true` (both confirmation paths, §1.2) | Service system |
| `verified_review` (4 stars) | 4 × trust weight | As above | Service system |
| `verified_review` (3 stars) | 0 | As above | Service system |
| `verified_review` (2 stars) | −4 × trust weight | As above | Service system |
| `verified_review` (1 star) | −8 × trust weight | As above | Service system |
| `portfolio_item` | 5 | `PortfolioItem.status = published`, media approved, customer consent `granted` or `not_required` | Vehicle + service system |
| `service_record` | 3 | `VehicleServiceRecord.verification_status` is `invoice_verified` or `admin_verified` | Vehicle + service system |
| `job_log` | 3 | `ProviderJobLog.verification_status` is not `self_reported` | Service system |
| `certification` | 15 | `ProviderCertification.verification_status = verified`, type manufacturer, industry or ev_high_voltage | Brand and/or service system from `scope_node_ids` |
| Self-declared claim | 0 | Never | — (cannot earn badges) |

Rules:
- **No double counting.** One real-world job counts once. If a job log, service record and appointment describe the same job, only the highest-weight item counts. A review on either path counts once per job; an approval-based review written for a job that already has a counted appointment is attached to it as evidence, not counted twice.
- Certifications of type `trade_license` or `safety` do not add capability evidence. They count toward Profile Integrity (§3.1).
- When a review is removed or a dispute against it is upheld, its evidence is set to `revoked` and scores are recalculated.

## 1.2 Review trust weight

Every published review is confirmed in one of two alternative ways (MVP Module F). There is no "unverified" review and no separate unverified section. `Review.trust_weight` by `verified_status`:

| Path | `verified_status` | Label shown | `trust_weight` | `counts_toward_core_score` |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| 1, visit-based | appointment_verified | Verified visit | 1.0 | true |
| 1, visit-based | inquiry_verified | Verified visit | 1.0 | true |
| 1, visit-based | service_record_verified | Verified visit | 1.0 | true |
| 1, visit-based | invoice_verified | Verified visit | 1.0 | true |
| 1, visit-based | fleet_confirmed | Verified visit | 1.0 | true |
| 1, visit-based | admin_verified | Verified visit | 1.0 | true |
| 2, approval-based | approved_without_visit | Approved by the business and CarPal | **0.6** (launch default, to be calibrated in Stage 7) | true |
| 2, approval-based | pending_approval | — (hidden; the author sees "Waiting for approval") | 0 | false |
| 2, approval-based | rejected | — (not published) | 0 | false |

Rules:
- **Path 1** needs a confirmed appointment that took place (or a completed stock inquiry, confirmed service record, invoice check or fleet confirmation). It is verified automatically after the safety and abuse checks. The shop does not approve it and cannot block it.
- **Path 2** needs both the business's confirmation of the relationship and a CarPal reviewer's approval (§9, item 7). It counts as evidence, toward the trust score and toward badges, at the lower weight. The weight only changes with a new `rule_version`.
- A review the business disputed and a CarPal reviewer upheld is published with the note "Disputed by the business" and keeps the Path 2 weight.
- **Count thresholds.** Where a badge or capability rule needs a number of counted reviews (for example "≥ 20 counted reviews"), each review counts by its `trust_weight`, so a Path 2 review counts as 0.6 of a review. Averages are always weighted by `trust_weight` and freshness.
- A rating is never a reason to reject an approval or to lower a weight.

# 2. Capability Confidence (Skill Heatmap)

## 2.1 Levels

Calculated per `ProviderCapability`:

`confidence_score = Σ (base_weight × freshness multiplier)` over its `active` evidence (freshness in §6).

| `confidence_level` | Level | `confidence_score` | Typical evidence | Public display |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| self_declared | 0 | < 10 | Provider claim only | "Offered" |
| low_evidence | 1 | 10–29 | 1–2 verified jobs or reviews | Listed, no tag |
| moderate_evidence | 2 | 30–79 | 3–7 verified jobs or reviews | "Experienced" tag |
| strong_evidence | 3 | 80–199 | 8–20 verified jobs or reviews + photos | "Specialist" badge eligible |
| verified_expert | 4 | ≥ 200, **and** ≥ 20 counted jobs, **and** ≥ 4.8 average rating in the category | 20+ jobs | "Expert" badge eligible |

`ProviderCapability.badge_eligible = true` at level 3 or higher.

## 2.2 AI's role and approvals

- **AI never sets a confidence level.** Levels are always computed by the worker from evidence rows.
- AI can **suggest a new capability** (e.g. from job logs or review themes). It is stored as an `AiInsight` + `AiSuggestion`. The capability row is created with `source = ai_suggested` and `status = pending_provider_approval`, and is not public until the provider approves it (MVP Module C).
- AI can **link existing evidence** to a capability (e.g. a verified review whose text is about suspension work). That row has `linked_by = ai_mapping` and `status = pending_approval`, so it does not count yet. If it would raise the capability's level, an `ApprovalRequest` with `action_type = upgrade_capability_level` is sent to the provider owner or manager. The evidence counts only after approval.
- Every AI capability or badge suggestion must cite the exact `ProviderCapabilityEvidence` IDs in `AiInsight.evidence_references` (§9).

## 2.3 Skill heatmap classification

`SkillHeatmapEntry.classification` (provider-private, MVP Module C):

| Classification | Rule |
|---|---|
| strong | Level 3+ and category rating ≥ 4.5 |
| emerging | Level 1–2 and `trend = rising` (score up ≥ 20% over 90 days) |
| weak | Level 0–1 with any negative review evidence, or category rating < 3.5 |
| opportunity | `local_demand_score` ≥ 60 and provider level ≤ 1 for that scope |

Heatmap entries require the provider's `provider_analytics_ai_use` consent. The explanation text is an `AiInsight`, but the numbers come from this protocol.

# 3. Organization Trust Score (0–100)

Stored in `Organization.trust_score` and as a `TrustSignal` of type `overall_trust_score`. Fleet organizations have no trust score.

## 3.1 Provider trust score

| Component | Weight | Inputs (TrustSignal types) |
|---|---:|---|
| **Review quality** | 40% | Average overall rating of counted reviews (both confirmation paths), weighted by `trust_weight` and freshness |
| **Operational reliability** | 30% | `request_response_rate`, `avg_response_time`, `completion_rate`, `provider_cancellation_rate` |
| **Profile integrity** | 15% | Verification status, `profile_completeness`, active verified locations, verified certifications (incl. trade licenses) |
| **Evidence volume** | 15% | Count of active counted evidence across all capabilities |

`customer_no_show_rate` is tracked but **never** lowers the provider's score, because `no_show` means the customer did not arrive.

## 3.2 Vendor trust score

| Component | Weight | Inputs |
|---|---:|---|
| **Review quality** | 40% | Average overall rating of counted vendor reviews, weighted as above |
| **Inquiry responsiveness** | 25% | `inquiry_fulfillment_rate`, `StockInquiry.first_response_at` average |
| **Catalog quality** | 20% | `catalog_quality_score` (§7.2) |
| **Profile integrity** | 15% | Verification status, `profile_completeness`, active verified locations |

A hybrid provider+vendor organization gets the average of both scores, weighted by each side's counted review volume.

## 3.3 Penalties

Applied after the weighted score, with a floor of 0.

| Trigger | Penalty | Source |
|---|---:|---|
| Negative review (1–2 stars) unanswered after 7 days | −5 each, max −20 | `ReviewResponse` missing |
| Provider cancels > 10% of confirmed appointments (last 90 days) | −10 | `provider_cancellation_rate` |
| Content or policy violation confirmed | −20 | `ModerationAction` on the organization's content |
| Fake review or self-review confirmed | −50 + all badges revoked | `Dispute` resolved_upheld or `ModerationAction` with reason `fake` |
| Manual admin penalty | Set by admin | `ModerationAction.action_type = apply_trust_penalty` → `admin_trust_penalty` signal |

Penalties expire after 12 months unless the admin sets otherwise.

# 4. Badges

Badges are strictly rule-based, recalculated nightly and on relevant events (`review.published`, `appointment.completed`, `organization.verified`, `moderation.action.taken`). Each award writes a `ProviderBadge` row with `rule_key`, `rule_version` and an `evaluation_snapshot` containing the inputs, thresholds and evidence IDs. There are no free-text custom badges.

| `badge_type` | MVP name | Unlock rule | Scope | Revoked or expired when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| verified_business | Verified Business | `business_identity` and `location` `OrganizationVerification` both verified. **Requires admin approval.** | — | Verification expires or is rejected; organization suspended |
| make_specialist | e.g. "BMW Bodywork Specialist" | Brand + system: a level 3+ capability for that brand and service system. Brand-wide: level 3+ in 3+ distinct service systems for that brand. | Brand (+ service system) | The qualifying capability drops to level 2 or below |
| system_expert | e.g. "Suspension Expert" | Level 4 in a service system (any brand) | Service system | Drops to level 3 or below |
| highly_rated | — (platform addition) | Average rating ≥ 4.8 over ≥ 20 counted reviews | — | Average < 4.8 or < 20 counted reviews |
| fast_responder | — (platform addition) | Average response time to appointment requests < 2 hours (last 30 days, ≥ 5 requests) | — | Average ≥ 2 hours |
| high_response_rate | High Response Rate | Responds (confirm, propose or decline) to ≥ 90% of appointment requests within 24 hours (last 90 days, ≥ 10 requests) | — | Rate < 90% |
| repeat_customer_favorite | Repeat Customer Favorite | ≥ 25% of completed appointments in the last 12 months are from returning customers, with ≥ 20 completed appointments and average rating ≥ 4.5 | — | Any condition fails |
| fast_appointment_turnaround | Fast Appointment Turnaround | Median time from `requested` to `confirmed_start_at` ≤ 48 hours (last 90 days, ≥ 10 confirmed appointments) | — | Median > 48 hours |
| excellent_communication | Excellent Communication | Average `communication` sub-rating ≥ 4.8 over ≥ 15 counted reviews (last 12 months) | — | Average < 4.8 or < 15 reviews |
| fleet_friendly | Fleet Friendly | ≥ 5 completed appointments with `requester_type = fleet` and no upheld fleet dispute | — | Revoked only by moderation |
| ev_ready | EV Ready | Verified `ev_high_voltage` certification **and** level 2+ in the EV battery/systems service system | EV service system | Certification expires; level drops below 2 |

All badges are revoked when a fake review or self-review is confirmed (§3.3) or the organization is suspended.

# 5. Ranking & Match Scores

Computed by the discovery module and stored on `MatchResult` (`match_score`, `confidence`, `reasons`). Every component produces at least one structured reason, so every ranked result can explain itself (MVP Module E, Plan WP8).

## 5.1 Provider match score

`match_score = (Capability fit × 0.40) + (Category trust × 0.30) + (Operational × 0.20) + (Distance × 0.10) + User preference bonus`

| Component | MVP matching factor | Calculation | `reason_type` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capability fit | Explicit fit + evidence fit | Confidence level for the exact brand / model / series / task requested; level 2+ scores high; self-declared scores near zero | capability_evidence |
| Category trust | Rating quality + trust signals | Trust score weighted toward counted reviews and evidence in the requested category | rating_evidence, trust_signal |
| Operational | Availability | Next available slot within the needed window, response time, acceptance rate | availability |
| Distance | Location | Straight-line distance to the origin of the search, within the provider's service radius (a circle in the MVP). The origin is the user's position, the chosen city, or the **map centre** after "Search this area"; the ranking rules do not change. | location |
| Freshness | Recency | Applied inside capability fit and category trust (§6) | recency |
| User preference bonus | User preference | +5 for a favorite; +5 for a fleet `preferred_provider`; total capped at +10 | user_preference |

Urgency: for `high` and `safety_critical` requests, the weights change to capability 0.35, trust 0.25, operational 0.30, distance 0.10, and an `urgency_fit` reason is added.

Filters (not scoring): fleet `preferred_provider_mode = strict` limits results to preferred partners. Providers with `activity_status = inactive` are always ranked last (§6).

## 5.2 Vendor match score (MVP §12 vendor ranking)

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Fitment confidence for the requested vehicle | 0.30 |
| Stock availability | 0.20 |
| Distance | 0.15 |
| Vendor rating | 0.15 |
| Response time | 0.10 |
| Category relevance | 0.10 |

Price visibility is a filter and tie-breaker, not a score component.

## 5.3 Inventory item score (MVP §12 inventory ranking)

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Fitment confidence (confirmed 1.0, likely 0.7, uncertain 0.3) | 0.35 |
| Stock status | 0.20 |
| Vendor rating | 0.15 |
| Distance | 0.10 |
| Data completeness (`data_quality_score`) | 0.10 |
| Price and condition fit to the user's filters | 0.10 |

## 5.4 Fairness rules

- **No hidden paid ranking.** Plan, payment, sponsorship and `featured_placement_eligible` are never inputs to any score. Featured placements are **not active in the MVP** (`featured.enabled` is off, so `MatchResult.is_sponsored` is always false). When they exist later they appear only in a separate slot labeled "Featured" (Plan §9 Rule 2) and **never on the map**.
- **Evidence over claims.** A provider with verified jobs in a category always outranks one with only self-declared claims in that category.
- **Explanation required.** Every result has structured `reasons`. The explanation text comes from AI (`match_explanation` insight) or, if AI is unavailable, from a template.
- **Same ranking everywhere.** A direct search, a Help Me recommendation and the map use the same scores and reasons. The map adds no new score: one pin per business location carries the rank, the reason summary and a `relevant_to_vehicle` flag (§5.5).

## 5.5 Search and map behaviour

- **Direct search without triage.** Free text is normalized and interpreted into a structured intent (target type, vehicle, service system or task, symptom, part category, shop name, location). Symptom-style text returns providers through the `symptom_to_system` and `symptom_to_task` mappings. Interpretation is rule-based first and works with AI off; an optional AI enhancer can only add suggestions. Help Me is offered, never forced.
- **Vehicle filter and highlight.** "For my car" keeps only results with evidence or fitment for the selected vehicle (`relevant_to_vehicle`). If fewer than 3 results would remain, the client says so and offers "Show all nearby". Advanced search highlights matching results and dims the others (they stay tappable); neither changes `match_score`.
- **Map pins.** One pin per business location. Vendor pins group their matching parts (`matching_item_count`) instead of one pin per part. Ranks 1–3 are numbered when the user comes from Help Me or sorts by best match. Public fields only. No featured pins.
- **Safety.** A safety keyword on the query or on request text sets a safety flag; it never hides or re-ranks results, except that urgent providers come first when the flag is `safety_critical`.

# 6. Freshness & Staleness

| Evidence or review age | Freshness multiplier |
|---|---:|
| 0–90 days | 1.5 |
| 91–365 days | 1.0 |
| 1–2 years | 0.5 |
| More than 2 years | 0.1 |

Age is measured from `ProviderCapabilityEvidence.evidence_date` (or the review's publish date).

**Staleness rule:** a provider with no completed appointments and no new counted reviews in 12 months gets `ProviderProfile.activity_status = inactive` and is ranked last regardless of historical score. It returns to `active` on the next completed appointment or counted review.

# 7. Vendor Inventory Trust & Fitment Confidence

## 7.1 Fitment confidence (`InventoryFitment.confidence`)

| Value | Weight in ranking | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| confirmed | 1.0 | Vendor confirmed it, or trusted structured data supports it later |
| likely | 0.7 | AI-suggested from part numbers or patterns; `status = pending_vendor_confirmation` |
| uncertain | 0.3 | Mapped only by broad category; the user is warned to verify |

## 7.2 Catalog quality score (0–100)

Stored in `VendorProfile.catalog_quality_score` and as a `catalog_quality_score` TrustSignal.

| Component | Weight | Measure |
|---|---:|---|
| Fitment coverage | 35% | % of active items with at least one `confirmed` fitment (`fitment_coverage`) |
| Data completeness | 35% | Average `InventoryItem.data_quality_score`: images, part numbers, category, warranty note, description, stock status |
| Inquiry fulfillment | 30% | Speed and outcome of `StockInquiry` responses (`inquiry_fulfillment_rate`) |

Return and defect rate ("incorrect fit" returns) is reserved for Phase 2, when commerce exists.

# 8. Fleet Trust Signals

Calculated only from appointments with `requester_type = fleet`, per provider. Stored as TrustSignals and used in fleet provider recommendations and the fleet analytics "preferred provider performance" report (MVP Module K).

| Signal | Calculation |
|---|---|
| `fleet_downtime_avg` | Average time from `checked_in` to `ready_for_pickup` (or `completed`), from `AppointmentStatusHistory` |
| `first_time_fix_rate` | % of fleet repairs where the same vehicle does not return for the same `service_task_node_id` within 30 days |
| `cost_estimate_accuracy` | 1 − average relative difference between `Appointment.cost_estimate_amount` and the final `VehicleServiceRecord.cost_amount` |

# 9. Governance, AI & Audit

1. **Evidence is immutable.** Once an appointment is `completed` or a review is `published`, the provider cannot change the evidence. Evidence can only be revoked (removal, upheld dispute).
2. **AI cannot invent evidence.** AI may calculate explanations and propose capabilities or evidence links, but it must cite exact evidence IDs, and its proposals only count after approval (§2.2). AI-derived signals (`ai_derived` group) inform moderation and insights but are not inputs to the trust score in the MVP.
3. **Admin overrides.** Admins apply trust penalties and revoke badges through `ModerationAction` (`apply_trust_penalty`, `revoke_badge`). These override algorithmic results and need a reason.
4. **Auditability.** Every trust score change of more than 5 points, and every badge award or revocation, writes an `AuditLog` entry with the evidence added or removed and the rule version. It also publishes `trust.score.changed`, `badge.awarded` or `badge.revoked`.
5. **Versioning.** `TrustSignal.rule_version` and `ProviderBadge.rule_version` record which version of this protocol produced each value.
6. **Kill switch.** The AI kill switch (`ai.automation.kill_switch`) stops AI evidence linking and AI explanations. Rule-based scoring keeps working. Search interpretation, the safety keyword check and the map are rule-based and keep working too.
7. **Review approval integrity (Path 2).** The business step is limited to confirming the customer relationship, so it cannot be used to hide honest criticism: a rejection opens a dispute decided by CarPal, silence for 7 days means no objection, and a rating is never a reason to reject. Business rejection, approval and the CarPal reviewer's decision are written to `AuditLog`. Review invitation links are single use, bound to one visit and one recipient, expire 7 days after sending, and need a one-time code, so they cannot be forwarded or reused to create Path 1 reviews. Invitation volume per provider is rate-limited and checked for self-review patterns.
8. **Trusted users (post-MVP).** The `UserAccount.trusted_user` flag only allows a limited approval queue later; it does not change any weight or score today.
