How it works

From the first strange noise to a repair you can trust.

  1. 1

    Add your car

    Brand, model and series, so matches fit your exact car.

  2. 2

    Describe the problem

    In your own words, in Persian or English. Not sure what's wrong? Ask Help Me.

  3. 3

    Get matched with proof

    Shops ranked by completed jobs and confirmed reviews, with the reasons shown.

  4. 4

    Book, then review

    Request an appointment. After the visit, your review helps the next driver.

Why you can trust what you see

Expertise needs evidence

A shop that is great at BMW bodywork won't be recommended for a Mercedes suspension problem unless it has proof of that work too.

Reviews come from real visits

A review is published after a confirmed appointment, or once both the business and CarPal approve it. A low rating is never a reason to reject one.

AI helps, people decide

AI helps you describe a problem and helps shops present their work. Nothing goes public until a person approves it.
