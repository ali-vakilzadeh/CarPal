Car owners

Fewer surprises, and a mechanic who knows your car

  • Search the way you talk

    Type in Persian or English, even something like "clunk over bumps". CarPal shows how it understood you, and you can correct it.

  • See why, every time

    Each recommendation says why it fits: jobs on your model, ratings for this kind of work, and availability.

  • Help Me, when you're not sure

    Answer a few questions to learn which system it may be, how urgent it is and who to see. Brake or steering problems get a clear safety warning.

  • Browse on the map

    See shops and parts sellers near you, filtered to your car, then open directions in your own map app.

  • Your car's history, kept safe

    Keep up to 5 cars for free, with service records, documents and reminders. It helps with diagnosis and when you sell.

  • Parts that fit

    Find parts for your exact car and ask the seller about stock before you make the trip.

Mechanics and shops

Be known for what you do best

  • A profile that proves your skill

    List the brands, models and jobs you handle. Finished jobs, before-and-after photos and reviews back it up.

  • Badges you earn

    Specialist badges come from completed jobs on CarPal, never from payment.

  • Requests that fit

    Customers are matched by car and problem, so you see more of the work you are good at. Each request arrives with a pre-visit report.

  • A calendar that protects your day

    Set your hours, slots and buffers. Reminders and status updates help cut no-shows and waiting.

  • Reviews that are fair to you

    Reviews are tied to real visits or confirmed customers. You can reply, report or open a dispute. AI can draft a reply, and you decide what gets published.

  • Showcases from your job photos

    AI pairs your before and after shots, suggests hiding plates and faces, and drafts a caption for you to approve.

Parts sellers

Stock that gets found by the right buyers

  • Your whole list in one upload

    Import a CSV or add items by hand. AI cleans the names, sorts categories, suggests which cars each part fits and spots duplicates. You confirm.

  • Found the way buyers search

    Owners and mechanics find you by car, part name or part number. On the map, one pin shows how many of your parts fit their car.

  • Real inquiries, not window shoppers

    Buyers ask about a specific part with their car details attached. Answer, or reserve it for them.

  • Your prices, your rules

    Keep public and trade prices apart. Only authorised trade buyers see your trade price.

  • Your catalogue stays yours

    Other sellers can't see your private data, and AI never passes it from one seller to another.

  • Know what to stock

    See your most searched parts, missing fitment data, and local demand you are not yet meeting.

The rules we keep for everyone

No fake expertise

Public specialist badges need evidence. AI can suggest a skill, but it only appears after the business confirms it and the proof is there.

No hidden paid ranking

Money can't quietly change who you are matched with, and paid placement never appears on the map.

Your data is yours

You own your car and repair data. Shops see it only when the service needs it, and your location is used only with your consent.
