Enterprise-Grade AI Governance & GDPR Compliance

The Problem with Standard AI

Hallucinations, data leaks, and unauthorized autonomous actions.

Resources (Read-Only)

AI can only see what it is allowed to see. Cannot read payment data to fix a brake issue.

Tools (Action-Oriented)

AI cannot autonomously publish content or book jobs. It creates Drafts. Humans must approve.

Immutable Audit Logs

Every prompt, every consent check, and every AI output is logged.

Result

Secure, scalable, multi-region AI infrastructure that guarantees GDPR compliance and builds absolute trust with users.

AI that Works Behind the Scenes to Drive Retention

Our AI isn't a chatbot gimmick; it is the operational backbone that reduces friction for B2B and B2C users.

For Providers

Skill Heatmaps: AI analyzes completed jobs, review sentiments, and portfolio uploads to automatically generate localized Skill Heatmaps.

For Vendors

Inventory Normalization: AI normalizes part names, categorizes them, and suggests vehicle fitments from messy CSVs.

For Fleets

Approval Workflows: AI analyzes driver reports, summarizes triage, estimates costs, and presents clean recommendations for one-click approval.

For Admin

Moderation: AI flags fraudulent reviews and PII before they reach the public feed.
